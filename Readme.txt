Textymizer
Offline document anonymization for privacy-conscious users.
Textymizer helps you remove sensitive information from documents locally on your computer before sharing them with AI tools, clients, colleagues, or third parties.
Your documents stay on your computer instead of being uploaded to an online anonymization service.

Why Textymizer?
Sharing documents with online services can expose information that was never meant to leave your computer.
Textymizer provides a local alternative for preparing documents before they are shared or processed elsewhere.

Key benefits
•	🔒 Local processing — document anonymization takes place on your computer.
•	📴 Offline-first privacy — your documents do not need to be uploaded to a remote server.
•	📄 Document anonymization — remove or anonymize sensitive information before sharing.
•	🤖 AI-ready workflow — prepare confidential documents before uploading them to AI services.
•	🖥️ Desktop application — work with documents directly on your own computer.
•	🎯 Privacy by design — minimize unnecessary exposure of sensitive information.

PDF Anonymization
PDF files often contain personal, confidential, or otherwise sensitive information.
Before sharing a PDF with another person, company, or AI service, you may want to remove information such as:
•	Names
•	Addresses
•	Phone numbers
•	Email addresses
•	Identification numbers
•	Client information
•	Internal references
•	Other sensitive information
Textymizer helps you prepare documents for safer sharing while keeping the processing local.

Anonymize Documents Before Using AI
AI tools can be extremely useful for analyzing, summarizing, translating, or extracting information from documents.
However, confidential documents may contain information that should not be shared with an external service.
A privacy-conscious workflow can look like this:
Your document
     ↓
Textymizer
     ↓
Sensitive information anonymized
     ↓
Anonymized document
     ↓
AI service / client / third party
Textymizer can therefore be used as a preparation step before sending documents to AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or other services.

Offline Processing
One of the core ideas behind Textymizer is simple:
Your documents should not have to leave your computer just to anonymize them.
Textymizer performs its document processing locally, allowing users to work with sensitive files without relying on an online document-upload service.
This can be particularly useful when working with:
•	Confidential business documents
•	Client information
•	Internal company documents
•	Personal documents
•	Documents containing personally identifiable information
•	Documents that should not be uploaded to third-party services

How It Works
A typical Textymizer workflow is:
1.	Open your document in Textymizer.
2.	Analyze the document for sensitive information.
3.	Review the information identified for anonymization.
4.	Select or confirm the information that should be anonymized.
5.	Create the anonymized document.
6.	Use the resulting document for sharing, analysis, or further processing.
The exact workflow and available features depend on the Textymizer version you are using.
